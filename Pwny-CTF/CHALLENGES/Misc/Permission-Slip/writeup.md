## Resolution method
1. **The idea**

Windows console game “SYSTEM BREACH”: hack 5 systems, but **all options fail**. The hint: “call `grant_admin_access()`” (winning function not achievable through normal play). Like [[Immortal-Kitten]], the flag displayed upon victory is **generated by an XOR routine** which is decrypted **statically**.

1. `strings` → `Here's your flag: `, `Congratulations, master hacker!`, hint `grant_admin_access()`. No clear flag.
2. **Disassemble** and locate the routine (@0x14000ac30): 8 byte key + ciphertext, loop `and eax,0x7` / `movzx r13d, byte[rbp+rax+0x48]` / `xor r13b, byte[rbp+r15-0x28]`.
3. **Extract**:
   - key: dwords `0x881fc35a`, `0xb1997e22` → `5a c3 1f 88 22 7e 99 b1`;
   - ciphertext: 8 dwords @`[rbp-0x28]` **+ 1 byte** `0x27` @`[rbp-0x8]` = **33 bytes** (do not forget the `mov BYTE PTR [rbp-0x8],0x27` after the dwords, otherwise the `}` is missing);
   - `flag[i] = ciphertext[i] XOR key[i & 7]`.
